Sony Pictures was the victim of a phishing attack in 2014, centered around accessing sensitive information such as the theft of employee personal data, leaking unreleases films and film scripts and the disclosure of sensitive email correspondences (S. De Tomas Colatin - NATO CCDCOE, 2014) Sony ceased all online activity and severed network connections whilst security policies were reviewed, after which questions around cyber security policies in the entertainment industry.

One limitation of the fix was there being a limited way to recoup the leaked information once it was published, the information was available on the internet where it could be accessed by anyone at any time.
